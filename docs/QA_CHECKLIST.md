# Production QA

## Before generation

- Every requested narration line is mapped; no silent rewriting or omitted ending.
- Historical sources support claims; legends, inference and reconstruction are distinguished.
- Recipe yield, quantities, dependencies, tools and realistic process times are specified.
- Period reenactment is distinct from modern test-kitchen work.
- Actor reference, visibility mode, sleeves/hands and kitchen are locked.
- Opening image depicts the pre-action state and contains all necessary props/destinations.
- Image/video agree on counts, positions, texture, tool and hand assignments.
- Each clip fits its duration at normal speed; one main delicate action per shot.
- Whipping/proofing/baking/fermentation use deliberate time jumps when required.
- ASMR corresponds to visible actions and materials; no silence/ASMR contradiction.
- Narration-line limits do not incorrectly shorten detailed prompt blocks.

## After generation (only claim checks actually performed)

- Hands, face and wardrobe remain consistent; only the intended actors appear.
- Tool contacts the food correctly; knife and stabilizing hand are safely separated.
- No utensil morphs, extra limbs, floating objects or food multiplication.
- Input/output ledger matches visible contents, amounts, waste and cooked state.
- Textures change plausibly; no liquid-to-finished-food morphing.
- Camera keeps decisive contacts visible and preserves spatial orientation.
- Generated audio has no accidental speech and matches actions, if listened to.
- Narration remains intelligible and approved audio is preserved.
- Actual visual/audio endpoints align; time jumps are clear in the edit.
- Failed assets are repaired locally; accepted assets are reused.

## Delivery truthfulness

State whether this is a prompt plan, generated assets or assembled/exported video. Distinguish text review, still-image inspection, motion review and listening. A completed render status alone does not establish visual or sound quality. Do not claim a repository was created until it exists and its files have been verified.
